# Product

## Register
brand

## Users and purpose
Visitors exploring SOMA00 robotic dexterity demonstrations and contacting SOMA.

## Brand direction
Preserve the approved SOMA00 demo site, typography, neutral palette, and existing logo. Show real robot demonstrations clearly.

## Design principles
Keep all three demonstrations visible in the final composition. Preview changes locally before publishing. Always compose and verify mobile separately rather than forcing desktop sizing.

## Accessibility
Respect reduced-motion preferences. Preserve keyboard access and readable video controls.
